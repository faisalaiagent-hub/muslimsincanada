# MIC discovery and promotion plan

Prepared 19 October 2026. No external account, paid campaign, partner outreach or third-party listing has been created by this work.

## First priorities

1. **Existing Facebook page:** start with the six feature posts and graphics in `assets/social/feature-campaign`. Pin an introductory feature post. Use page insights to choose times, respond to local recommendations and invite accurate listing suggestions. Three posts per week is a proposed starting cadence, not a scheduled campaign.
2. **Local mosque and community-centre newsletters:** seek a short MIC introduction and a link from an existing resources page. Start with Stouffville and Markham relationships, then expand through the GTA. Dar-ul-Khair and Dar-ul-Iman are suggested outreach prospects because the owner named them as nearby mosques; no partnership is implied. Confirm the right public contact before any outreach.
3. **GTA Facebook groups:** identify active Stouffville/Markham resident groups, Muslim family groups and local business groups. Ask the admins or follow each group’s promotion rules. Adapt the prepared introduction to a useful local feature rather than repeating identical posts across many groups.
4. **University and college Muslim student associations:** offer a newcomer/nearby-services guide for their welcome pages or newsletters. York, University of Toronto and Toronto Metropolitan associations are research prospects, not confirmed partners. Verify their current contacts and group rules before outreach.
5. **Business-owner referrals:** invite existing MIC advertisers to share their public listing and the marketplace guide with their customers. Build useful listings before investing in advertising.

## Places to evaluate next

- **Muslim Link:** established Canadian community news, events and directory platform. Explore an appropriate organization listing or a mutually useful introduction; paid advertising is a separate choice. [Platform](https://muslimlink.ca/) · [Advertising information](https://muslimlink.ca/advertise/).
- **Muslim business directories:** investigate whether MIC qualifies for a truthful relevant category. One possible submission route is [Muslimlinks.ca](https://www.muslimlinks.ca/add-your-business/). Review current eligibility and listing quality first; avoid bulk directory submissions.
- **Nextdoor Canada:** potentially useful for local awareness if MIC meets the platform’s business-page eligibility. Its Canadian business site offers business pages and updates. [Official Canadian business information](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-ca). Do not create an inaccurate physical location.
- **Instagram:** reuse the six square graphics if the owner wants to launch and maintain an account. Prioritize existing Facebook audience and partner referrals first.
- **LinkedIn:** consider only if business-owner recruitment and volunteering become a priority. Not needed just to accumulate another account.
- **WhatsApp community sharing:** provide the public guide links to interested community organizers who choose to distribute them. Do not import contacts or add people to groups without their consent.

## Search work still requiring account access or owner action

Connect Google Search Console to `https://www.muslimsincanada.com`, verify ownership, submit `/sitemap.xml` and review indexing. Bing Webmaster Tools is a second option. No search-console verification or submission is claimed by this release. Follow Google’s [sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap) and [SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).

Search engines decide whether and when to index pages. No rankings are promised. Add privacy-appropriate analytics and event measurement before reporting website conversions. Do not create a Google Business Profile for an online-only portal: Google lists online-only businesses as ineligible in its [eligibility guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036).
